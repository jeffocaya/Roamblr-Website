Our Story

Started with a
Saturday Walk

When Jeff Ocaya moved from Kitgum to Kampala, he started doing something simple. Every weekend he would follow a road until he found something extraordinary. Kabaka's Lake hiding inside the city. Buziga Hill where Kampala looks entirely different from above. A boat on Lake Victoria with a fisherman who knew things no guidebook ever would.

He posted the photos. His phone started buzzing. Friends asked where, how much, can I come next time? He realised he had become the person his circle trusted with the question: "where should I go?"

Roamblr Safaris is the professional answer to that question — built on genuine local knowledge, personal curiosity, and an honest belief that Uganda is one of the most extraordinary places on earth.
