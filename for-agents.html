What We're Measured On

Agencies evaluate ground handlers on six criteria. We've built Roamblr around all of them.

Response Speed

24-hour quote turnaround on standard itineraries. Urgent requests prioritized. You're never left waiting.

Quote Accuracy

Permit prices, lodge rates, fuel costs — all based on current supplier pricing, not guesses. Updated weekly.

Trip Communication

Pre-travel updates at 6 weeks, 2 weeks, 48 hours. Daily WhatsApp updates during the trip. You're always informed.

Problem-Solving

Vehicle breakdown? Lodge overbook? We have backups for everything. Crisis protocol in place. You never get a panicked call.

Financial Reliability

Clear invoicing. Deposits only used to secure your client's bookings. Transparent about where every dollar goes.

Documentation

Professional invoices, booking confirmations, pre-travel packs. Everything you need to present to your client.
