Why Agencies Choose Roamblr

We Solve the Problems
Other Operators Create

Every item on this list came from conversations with agencies about what they have experienced with Uganda ground handlers in the past.

The Problem

Slow Quote Turnaround

Your client is waiting. The other Uganda operator takes three days to respond. You lose the booking.

Roamblr guarantees a detailed quote within 24 hours on all standard itineraries. No exceptions.

The Problem

No Communication During Trips

Your client is in Uganda. You are in London. You hear nothing until they are back home — or until something goes wrong.

You receive a WhatsApp update every morning of every active trip. Three sentences. Everything is fine, or the problem is being solved.

The Problem

Permit Delays and Errors

Gorilla permits booked through a third-party middleman. Wrong dates. Wrong names. Delays that leave your client without a permit on trek day.

Roamblr holds a direct UWA operator account. We book permits ourselves, immediately on receiving deposit. No middlemen. No delays.

The Problem

Unprofessional Invoicing

Invoices arrive without your booking reference, without a TIN, in the wrong currency, three days late. Your accounts department rejects them.

Every Roamblr invoice references your booking number, includes our TIN, is in your agreed currency, and arrives within 48 hours of trip completion.

The Problem

Full in Peak Season

Your Uganda ground handler is fully booked in July. You have a client. They tell you three weeks before departure. You are left scrambling.

We tell you honestly when we are at capacity. We also maintain a bench of trusted backup suppliers so we can always deliver even in peak season.

The Problem

Niche Requests Refused

A client wants specialist birding. A documentary crew needs filming permits. A diaspora group wants a roots tourism itinerary. The standard operator says no.

We maintain specialist birding guides, filming permit contacts, and community tourism relationships for exactly these requests. We say yes.

Agency FAQ

Your agency remains liable to the end client for the full travel experience — this is the standard arrangement in the industry. Roamblr Safaris is liable to your agency for our delivery of the ground services we have confirmed. We carry liability insurance and operate under UTB licensing. We recommend all clients carry comprehensive travel insurance covering medical evacuation.

We accept international bank transfer (SWIFT), Wise transfer (preferred — lower fees for you), and for larger agencies in established relationships, we can discuss net-30 payment terms after three completed bookings. We do not accept credit cards directly but can discuss arrangements via payment platforms if needed.

We contact you immediately — before the client has a chance to. We maintain backup suppliers in every park area for vehicle breakdowns, lodge overbooking situations, and similar. In our experience, every problem that arises has a solution if you move fast enough. We move fast. You will hear about the solution at the same time you hear about the problem.
