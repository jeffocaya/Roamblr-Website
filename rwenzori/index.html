What's included

  • Camping / basic lodging, every night on route
  • 3 basic meals a day along the way
  • Shared luggage transport & mechanical backup
  • A scouted route, ridden with an experienced guide

Not included

  • Marathon registration — UGX 60,000, register yourself at rwenzorimarathon.com
  • Return transport, Kasese → Kampala/Entebbe

  • Personal spending money
Who this is for

Ugandan cyclists and runners who can comfortably ride 70–100 km a day, several days in a row, on a loaded bike — tarmac and rough back roads both. This is steady, sociable riding, not a race. But it is genuinely physical, and if you're also lining up for the marathon, you should already be running regularly.
