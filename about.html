Our Story

Some businesses start
with a business plan.
Roamblr started
with a Saturday.

The honest story of how one man's curiosity about his own country became a safari company serving travelers from around the world — and Ugandan families discovering their own backyard.

01

The Arrival

When Jeff Ocaya moved from Kitgum to Kampala, he did what most people do in a new city. He worked. He found his routine. He learned the streets.

But on Saturdays, he left.

Not far. Not expensively. Just somewhere that was not his apartment and not his office. A new city. A new life. The search for something that felt like his in a place that did not yet feel like home.

There was no plan. No business model. Just a man, a city he was still learning, and a quiet resolution to follow his curiosity wherever it led — one Saturday at a time.

02

The Discovery

One Saturday he walked out of his door in central Kampala, picked a road at random, and said to himself: I am going to follow this road until I find something interesting.

Two hours later he was standing outside Rubaga Cathedral — and thinking about his father, whose devotion to the Catholic church had defined his childhood in Kitgum. He went inside, knelt down, and prayed. Tears on his face. A son talking to God on behalf of a man he missed completely.

He had left home looking for something interesting. He had not expected to find something true.

Kabaka's Lake

Hiding inside Kampala like a secret the city was keeping from itself. A massive man-made lake, right in the middle of the capital, that most residents had never seen.

Buziga Hill

Where the whole of Kampala spread out below him and looked entirely different from above. A perspective shift available to anyone willing to climb.

Gaba Beach

Where Lake Victoria stretched to a horizon so flat and wide it looked like the beginning of an ocean. A fisherman pressed his contact number into Jeff's hand.

Rubaga Cathedral

Where grief and faith met unexpectedly. A random walk turned into a moment of genuine connection with a father who was no longer there.

“
He had left home looking for something interesting. He had not expected to find something true.
”
03

The Ripple

Jeff posted the photos on his WhatsApp status. No strategy. No content calendar. Just a man sharing what he'd found because it was genuinely interesting.

Then his phone started buzzing.

A friend went on his own micro adventure and called to share it — excited, grateful, transformed. A mother with young children asked for somewhere affordable to take her kids. Another friend said simply: "Next time you go, I am coming."

Jeff had not started a business. He had just been following his curiosity one Saturday at a time — and without meaning to, had become the person his circle trusted with the question: where should I go?

04

The Purpose

Roamblr Safaris is the professional answer to that question. Built on the same curiosity that took Jeff to Kabaka's Lake on a quiet Saturday. Scaled to serve families, honeymooners, international travelers, and overseas agencies.

We are a Kampala-based safari and ground handling company arranging Uganda travel experiences for international tour agencies, Ugandan families, honeymooners, school groups, corporate teams, and direct travelers worldwide.

We handle gorilla trekking permits, multi-park safari logistics, weekend getaways, Kampala day trips, and everything in between.

Uganda is extraordinary. Most people — including most Ugandans — have no idea how extraordinary. We intend to fix that.

Our Values

What Guides Everything We Do

These aren't corporate values written for a wall poster. They're the principles that emerged naturally from how Roamblr was built — one genuine interaction at a time.

Genuine Curiosity

We don't sell experiences we haven't personally explored. Every destination in our packages is somewhere we've genuinely discovered and believe in. Curiosity is our business model.

24-Hour Responsiveness

When a client emails at 10pm, they have a full quote by the next morning. When things go wrong during a trip, we answer the phone. Speed of response is a competitive advantage that costs nothing.

Local First, Always

Every guide is Ugandan. Every lodge we recommend is personally vetted. Every community tourism experience puts money directly into local hands. Uganda's heritage belongs to Ugandans first.

Honest Pricing

No hidden fees. No bait-and-switch accommodations. We build a 5-8% buffer into every quote so we never have to surprise a client with an unexpected cost. What we quote is what you pay.

Sustainable Operations

No single-use plastic on any trip. Community tourism built into every multi-day package. Wildlife viewing protocols enforced. We're building toward GSTC certification because the gorillas can't advocate for themselves.

Reviews Over Advertising

We'd rather have five genuine TripAdvisor reviews than a thousand paid ads. Every booking is an opportunity to earn a public recommendation. Our best marketing is a client who can't stop talking about their experience.
